# Variant C style spec ("faces")

Sampled from `ref/owner-pick.png`, `ref/dragonbee-f1.jpg`, `ref/dragonbee-f2.jpg`, `ref/dragonbee-f3.jpg` and `ref/dragonbee-thumb.jpg` (median-cut quantisation plus point samples). Pixel sizes below are at 1920x1080.

1. **Two worlds, one idiom.** Warm "day/court" scenes are sepia to blood red. Cool "night/divine" scenes are indigo to violet. Each frame stays inside one family. The only exceptions are a single saturated red accent, and Ganga herself, whose pale face and indigo cloth carry the cool family into the warm frame.
2. **Warm palette** (owner-pick, f1). Silhouette ink `#060201` to `#150000`. Deep cloud `#2f1615`. Cloud `#5a2724`. Mid wall `#76453e`, `#9f5647`, `#a96954`. Light wall `#bd7f67`, `#cf967a`. Hot centre `#e0ac86` to `#e6b58d`. Top-edge darkening `#a54b3a`. Line cream `#f2c7a0` (brightest line pixels `#fdca9d` to `#ffd6a7`; anti-aliased they read about `#e0b090`).
3. **Accent red** `#d7071d` (mean of all saturated red pixels in f1). Exactly one red object per warm frame, almost always a thin arrow on a diagonal.
4. **Cool palette** (f2, thumb, f3). Night black `#0b0e15` to `#121340`. Indigo `#22255b`, `#2c2f6b`, `#2d2352`. Violet `#403273`, `#504184`, `#624e94`. Lilac `#705f8a`, `#8ba0d8`. Pale `#bfd2ef`. Blossom white `#fcf8f9`.
5. **Pale face.** Fill `#fbf7f8` in the centre, falling to `#e2d9e6` at the edges where the hair shadows it. Outline and feature ink `#5c4a66`. Blush is a soft horizontal band `#e8a9bf` across both cheeks and the nose bridge, never two round dots. Neck sits in shadow `#bba6b5`.
6. **Silhouette rule.** Dark characters are flat ink with no interior shading at all. Form comes only from cream hairlines. Line weight is uniform, about 3 to 4 px on bodies and 2.5 to 3.5 px on faces. Lines end bluntly (round caps) and never taper into brush strokes.
7. **Line density.** Sparse. A chest-up king carries about 15 to 25 lines: a vertical centre seam, two symmetrical collar V pairs crossing low on the chest, diagonal sash pairs, one inner sleeve line per arm, and the face. Large areas of pure ink are left empty on purpose.
8. **Shape language.** Tall, narrow, rectilinear. The king's crown, head, beard and neck form one tall column that flares in concave curves into very broad, square shoulders (shoulder span about 4.8 head widths). The princess face is a long shield. It is wide and straight at the temples and drops to a pointed chin, about 1.45 times as tall as wide.
9. **Dark face (king).** All acting happens in cream. The eyes are narrow lit almond slits filled with cream and a dark pupil dot, the upper lid line running out past the corner. The brows are short angled strokes with hooked ends. The nose is a small wedge. The mustache has spiral-curled tips. A forehead mark sits between the brows (made a Vaishnava U tilak here). Expression moves the brow angle, the slit height, the mustache rotation and the mouth under the mustache.
10. **Pale face (princess).** The eyes are large, simple almonds with a heavy dark upper-lid stroke and a thin crease line above. Inside sits a big iris (dark centre, muted mauve rim) with one white catch-light, top left. The brows are thin, long and high, well above the eyes. The nose is a single long line from between the eyes to a small hook. The mouth is tiny, with a filled mauve upper lip and a short lower-lip stroke.
11. **Hair.** Dark violet-black `#2e2438` panels frame the face tightly from a centre part and cover the outer forehead and temples. Sparse lighter strand lines `#6a5a80` follow the panels.
12. **Swirl clouds.** Each cloud is a mass of overlapping round lobes, filled tone-on-tone and darker than the sky. It has a thin outline only on its outer scalloped edge, plus one spiral curl per lobe (about 1.3 turns, curling inward). Its lines are a lighter tone of the same hue, not cream. A soft dark halo sits under each mass. Clouds sit at the top corners and frame the figure.
13. **Sky.** A radial glow, hottest just behind the figure's head and shoulders, falling to darker red at the top and edges. Broad, faint diagonal light bands suggest painted brush streaks.
14. **Grain.** One whole-frame monochrome fine grain layer at low strength, overlay-blended. It gives the slightly speckled, printed texture of the refs. No blur filters on elements.
15. **Night ornament.** Indigo backgrounds carry darker tone-on-tone growth. Here that means curving lotus stems and pads in place of f2's thorns, with pale blossom shapes (lotus buds and petals) as the only bright points. Fine mandala and dot rings, as in the thumb, become Ganga's halo.
16. **Indian ornament, translated.** Temple-border sawtooth bands, a lotus finial, keri (paisley) clasps and a dotted haar necklace, all drawn as the same sparse hairlines. Nothing is filled with colour and nothing has miniature-painting detail.
17. **Composition.** The figure is central and frontal or near frontal, and cropped low (chest-up shots cut at the frame bottom). The head sits in the upper third, against the brightest part of the sky. The diagonal accent crosses from upper left to lower right through the hands.
18. **Acting.** The faces read at phone size by changing only a few big signals: brow angle, eye opening, mouth curve or opening, and blush. Features never gain extra lines to emote. A blink is the upper lid closing onto the lower lid line.
